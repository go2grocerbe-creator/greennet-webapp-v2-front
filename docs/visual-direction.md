# Visual Direction

## Desired character

- Clean
- Modern
- Warm
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Quietly premium
- Mobile-first

## Methods

Use typography, spacing, proportion, editorial imagery, clear hierarchy and
restrained transitions. Keep navigation familiar and product discovery direct.

## Avoid

- 3D scenes and WebGL
- scroll hijacking
- excessive parallax
- novelty cursors
- animated backgrounds
- neon effects
- excessive glassmorphism
- dense card grids
- decorative motion without a usability purpose

The CSS tokens in the scaffold are provisional structure, not the final GreenNet
brand palette.
